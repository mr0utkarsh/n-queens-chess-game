How to Play N-Queens

The N-Queens Puzzle is a classic chess puzzle. The objective is to place N queens on an N × N chessboard such that no two queens threaten each other.

Conflict Rules:

No two queens can share the same:

  • Horizontal Row
  • Vertical Column
  • Diagonal Line

💡 Tip: Use the "Threat Zones" toggle to see red highlight lines representing queen sightlines!
